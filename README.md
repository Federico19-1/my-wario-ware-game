# Wario Loves Taralli!
A WarioWare-like game where Wario is obsessed by the tipical south italian buscuit (only for PC)

You can try it here: https://federico19-1.itch.io/wario-loves-taralli

## How to play
You have 5 lives and you have to win at least 6 times to achieve the win.
The minigames are:
- 1. Platformer Game: You have to grab all 3 Sicilian Taralli in less of 10 seconds;
- 2. Clicker Game: You have to click at all 5 Napoletain Taralli in less of 10 seconds;
- 3. Click it Game: You have to click multiple times at the Apulian Tarallo in less of 10 seconds;
- 4. DON'T Click it Game: You have to NOT click at the Apulian Tarallo or you lose, so you have to stay still for the rest of the minigame.

Game built using Godot Engine All assets used in the game are open source
